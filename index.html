The story hanging over local TV

  • The Republican Party of Florida has dropped its lawsuit seeking to block a Democratic ad about the Hope Florida controversy from Orlando and Tampa Bay-market TV stations. The party filed a voluntary dismissal on Wednesday, Oct. 21, one day after Highlands County Circuit Judge Peter Estrada refused its emergency request to pull the ads without a hearing. The dismissal is without prejudice, so the party could file again. Sources: Florida GOP drops Hope Florida ad lawsuit, GOP drops Hope Florida ad lawsuit, but prosecutors' warnings still stand
  • The threat to stations has not fully lifted: on Oct. 16, the same day the party sued, two state attorneys — Brian Haas of the Tenth Circuit and Susan Lopez of the 13th Circuit in Hillsborough — sent their own letters to TV station managers citing the grand jury secrecy statute and urging them to talk to their lawyers. No station has been charged. Source: Florida Man News Network update
  • Central Florida Public Media's Oct. 19 report on the original suit names the Orlando-market owners involved, including WFTV, WESH, WKMG, and WOFL. Source: Florida Republican Party wants court to block Democrats' Hope Florida ads

On radio: people moves

  • Cox Media Group Orlando has announced lineup changes at WWKA (K92.3) and WMMO, effective immediately. Slater moves from PM drive back to mornings, while Melissa Quinones, now K92.3 music director, moves from middays to cover PM drive. Jay Edwards, a 20-year market veteran most recently morning producer at sister station WDBO, becomes full-time midday talent on WWKA and PM drive talent on WMMO. Source: CMG Orlando Announces Programming Lineup Changes

The story hanging over local TV

  • The Republican Party of Florida has sued the owners of 11 stations in the Orlando and Tampa Bay markets, seeking to block a Democratic ad about the Hope Florida controversy and threatening criminal prosecution if stations air material from a leaked grand jury report.
  • Press-freedom groups say the suit threatens broadcasters' First Amendment rights, while Republicans point to an Oct. 7 court ruling ordering the grand jury report removed from court records.
  • Source: Florida GOP Sues 11 TV Stations to Block Democratic Ad

On radio: people moves

  • Audacy has named CJ Robinson brand manager of Orlando's Mix 105.1 (WOMX). He joined the station in 2012 and continues to host Mix Mornings with CJ & Jenn. Source: Orlando's Mix Gets a Homegrown Brand Manager in CJ Robinson
  • Cox Media Group's WOEX in Orlando has added Liliani Hernandez as midday host. Source: Liliani Hernandez Joins WOEX/Orlando as New Midday Host
  • WOEX has also been relaunched as "Hits 96.5," playing English pop from the 80s, 90s, and 2000s with Spanish-speaking hosts — Epi in mornings, Liliani middays, Luis Angel afternoons, and Martica at night. Source: CMG Radio Orlando Unveils New Spanish Format
  • On the talk side, iHeartMedia Orlando's NewsRadio WFLF (540 AM) added The Ryan Gorman Show with Ryan Gorman and Dana McKay to weekday mornings, 6-9 a.m., originating from Tampa's WFLA. Source: The Ryan Gorman Show Expands To Orlando
