Central Florida Airwaves — Saturday, October 24, 2026

Saturday, October 24, 2026

TL;DR

  • Solar Bears hockey is on free over-the-air TV tonight on WRDQ-TV Magic TV 27.
  • The Florida GOP lawsuit against 11 Orlando/Tampa Bay-market TV stations remains the biggest story hanging over local TV.
  • On radio: CJ Robinson is now brand manager at Mix 105.1, and WOEX has relaunched as Hits 96.5 with Liliani Hernandez in middays.

On TV tonight

  • The Orlando Solar Bears are on free over-the-air TV tonight: vs. South Carolina at 7:00 p.m. on WRDQ-TV Magic TV 27, as part of a new 19-game deal with Cox Media Group for the 2026-27 season.
  • Source: Orlando Solar Bears to Broadcast 19 Games on Magic TV 27

The story hanging over local TV

  • The Republican Party of Florida has sued the owners of 11 stations in the Orlando and Tampa Bay markets, seeking to block a Democratic ad about the Hope Florida controversy and threatening criminal prosecution if stations air material from a leaked grand jury report.
  • Press-freedom groups say the suit threatens broadcasters' First Amendment rights, while Republicans point to an Oct. 7 court ruling ordering the grand jury report removed from court records.
  • Source: Florida GOP Sues 11 TV Stations to Block Democratic Ad

On radio: people moves

  • Audacy has named CJ Robinson brand manager of Orlando's Mix 105.1 (WOMX). He joined the station in 2012 and continues to host Mix Mornings with CJ & Jenn. Source: Orlando's Mix Gets a Homegrown Brand Manager in CJ Robinson
  • Cox Media Group's WOEX in Orlando has added Liliani Hernandez as midday host. Source: Liliani Hernandez Joins WOEX/Orlando as New Midday Host
  • WOEX has also been relaunched as "Hits 96.5," playing English pop from the 80s, 90s, and 2000s with Spanish-speaking hosts — Epi in mornings, Liliani middays, Luis Angel afternoons, and Martica at night. Source: CMG Radio Orlando Unveils New Spanish Format
  • On the talk side, iHeartMedia Orlando's NewsRadio WFLF (540 AM) added The Ryan Gorman Show with Ryan Gorman and Dana McKay to weekday mornings, 6-9 a.m., originating from Tampa's WFLA. Source: The Ryan Gorman Show Expands To Orlando

Public radio watch

  • Central Florida Public Media, which operates 90.7 WMFE in Orlando and 89.5 WMFV in The Villages, has added Sam Stockbridge as elections reporter ahead of the midterms, covering elections and investigative stories after covering Texas redistricting. Source: Central Florida Public Media Adds Elections Reporter

Industry note

  • Orlando is among the markets where the Media Rating Council denied accreditation for Nielsen's Audio PPM service in its Oct. 16 quarterly report. Losing or being denied accreditation doesn't stop Nielsen from selling the service, but some agencies weigh the audited designation when buying time. Source: MRC pulls Nielsen PPM accreditation in four radio markets

Orlando TV at a glance

  • WESH (NBC), WKMG (CBS), WFTV (ABC), WOFL (Fox), WRDQ, WKCF (CW), and WUCF (PBS) were among the local NEXTGEN TV launch stations. Source: TV stations in Orlando launch NEXTGEN TV broadcasts
